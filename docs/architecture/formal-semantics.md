# Operational semantics

Let H=(C,Q,W,r,J), where C maps names to versioned components, Q is the inactive/quarantined set, W is a monotone set of withdrawn (name,version) pairs, r is a revision, and J is an audit chain. D is the union of dependency and provenance edges. Policy P and validator registry V are operator-owned and immutable for an engine instance.

A proposal e contains target t, expected version v, transformation op, and required validators. Analysis constructs an isolated H'. Update requires v=C[t].version and a strictly increasing new version; withdrawal adds (t,v) to W and quarantines t and reverse reachability D*(t); recovery loads a known historical component and rejects W conflicts. Dependencies and provenance must be acyclic and active for every active artifact. Descendants of changed content are quarantined. Protected descendants make a transformation invalid when they would be quarantined.

Lifecycle: PROPOSED → ANALYSED → VERIFIED → APPROVED → DEPLOYED, with REJECTED from any predeployment step. APPROVED → REVALIDATION_REQUIRED if r changed. DEPLOYED components can become QUARANTINED or WITHDRAWN through a later evolution. Proposal lifecycle and component trust state are separate.

Approval binds hash(e), hash(H), hash(P), and validator results. Commit under a lock requires an unconsumed capability and the identical H fingerprint. It checks candidate invariants, stages the audit event, and atomically swaps the sandbox reference. An injected adapter failure before the swap leaves H and approval unchanged. Revalidation is a new checked update or rollback of a quarantined component; descendants remain quarantined until their own revalidation.

Version 0.2 strengthens this model with immutable version history K and transitive version-origin map L. An update inherits the old target identity and its origins, plus current parent identities and their origins. Recovery restores the exact historical origins. No active artifact's own identity or transitive origins can intersect W, even if a parent name now resolves to an older clean version. Parent removal and independent release remain unsupported. Protected kinds policy/verifier are isolated even when their mutable flag is true.

Approval also binds the validator registry, candidate, origins and complete context (state, policy, registry, history, origins). The context is captured before validation and checked after every validator and at commit. Journal v2 stores source, IR digest, principal, policy and registry fingerprints, origins, evidence, and pre/post-state hashes; anchored replay re-executes the source against the trusted policy and validators.
