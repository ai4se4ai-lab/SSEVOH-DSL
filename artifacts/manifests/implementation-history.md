## Current operational limitations

Cluster queue times are external and can exceed a chat turn. The finite controller runs for up to 180 cycles at a minimum 120-second interval (plus audit and transfer time), refreshes evidence, and may submit the authorized full study only after qualification. Its 90-minute qualification deadline makes still-unqualified sites explicit; it never invents their performance. At least one site must pass all qualification gates before an assignment exists. Failure or mismatched source/model identity requires attention. The monitor stopping does not cancel Slurm jobs.

Five empirical synthetic mechanism-removal variants plus a full control are implemented separately from the five bounded lifecycle mutants. All full-control decisions/states pass locally; four removals exposed errors under the earlier core; current rollback-origin defense overlaps the removed historical guard and withdrawal-validation removal has zero observed effect because other guards remain. Scheduled CPU smoke jobs qualify this extension on all three sites; full repeated results remain pending. Exact removal scope and overlapping defenses are documented in experiments/ABLATIONS.md. Human usability, durable recovery, broad benchmark representativeness and production semantics are also unestablished. These are outstanding research tasks, not completed phases.

The original Rorqual GPU-discovery failure is retained under artifacts/manifests/runtime-v1-failure. A second protocol stages identical runtime libraries on node-local disk and checks model residency before any task prompt; qualification was restarted consistently on all sites. All five model tags passed GPU loading and blob-integrity preflight on Fir; their full identities are frozen in hpc/config/models.frozen.json. This is preflight evidence, not the 500-generation model evaluation. Three successful CPU ablation-smoke artifacts are retained under artifacts/benchmark-results/hpc-ablation-smoke.

A late regression review found that historical recovery also needs to check restored capabilities against the current trusted ceiling. The candidate engine and independent concrete verifier now enforce that guard, with two dedicated regression tests. Full-run source snapshots include this correction; GPU qualification characterizes the unchanged node-local runtime/model protocol rather than claiming an implementation refinement proof. The benchmark task and model matrices are unchanged.
