\section{Cluster experiments}
Fir and Rorqual used H100 allocations and Narval A100 allocations. Both current studies submitted three independent qualification graphs per site. Fir and Rorqual qualified before the 45-minute selection deadline; Narval remained queued then and its late observations are retained separately. The frozen plans selected Rorqual for the lower observed end-to-end completion cost, subject to model-cache constraints. This does not establish a universal hardware ranking. Source snapshots, accounting, logs, GPU traces and transfer hashes support each observation.

At least three independent complete smoke runs per model/site are required before that site is eligible for selection. Repeated generations within one allocation do not constitute independent queue trials. A site with an incomplete or failed run is not assigned an invented throughput or reliability result. Failed trials remain part of reliability accounting.

\section{Collected measurements and interpretation}
The collected five-model tables include syntax/semantic validity, sample sizes, unsafe generated proposals, unsafe deployed proposals, safe acceptance, false rejection, task compliance, latency and tokens. Per-scenario outcomes and raw outputs remain necessary to explain aggregate differences. Scaling tables must retain shape, size, repetition, latency, completion and peak traced Python allocation values. GPU memory, utilization, queue and job resource consumption come from recorded telemetry, not model parameter counts.

The implemented formal mutants remove five lifecycle guards. Separately, five synthetic sandbox ablations omit dependency closure, provenance checks, withdrawal validation, rollback conflict checks or revalidation, with precise scope documented in experiments/ABLATIONS.md. Local checks and qualification remain distinct from full repeated cluster measurements. These conditions do not establish mechanism effects on realistic artifact contents.

\section{Matched cross-site smoke observations}
The scheduled report compared 24 source/model/prompt/decoding/runtime-matched pairs, including completed cells from partially censored Narval trials. All raw texts and admission decisions matched, and both outputs in every pair were syntax-invalid. This is reproducibility of a protocol failure, not useful model behavior or full-benchmark replication. The report records three trial slots per site; not every slot completed both model cells. The comparison uses one shared first context and three seeds. Completed partial observations never alter the frozen deadline-based assignment.
